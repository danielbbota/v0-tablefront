# tablesfront.com website

Next.js / React site on Vercel. Marketing site for TableFront: websites for businesses that sell directly to customers with one clear action (book, order, buy) — hospitality is the flagship vertical, plus health & beauty (salons, barbers, clinics) and small producers & shops. Written from the perspective of someone who ran a restaurant.

## Rules
- Languages: EN, DE, PT (pt-PT, never Brazilian), FR. Currency switches by language: EN and PT show EUR, DE and FR show CHF, with a manual override selector (`lib/i18n/config.ts`).
- Brand: espresso #1A1208, cream #F5EFE0, brass gold #C9954A, terracotta #B85C38, off-white #EDE8DC. Fonts: DM Serif Display (headings — chosen for its conventional ampersand), Lora, Syne.
- Site structure: the homepage is ten sections (hero, business chooser, why, calculator, process, work, pricing summary, about, FAQ, free prototype). Say each thing once: the full plan cards live only on `/pricing`, and each business has one page — `/restaurants`, `/stays`, `/health-beauty`, `/shops` (`lib/business.ts`), reached from the chooser, the "For your business" menu and the footer. `/reservations` redirects to `/restaurants`.
- **One section per screen.** On a laptop every section must fit one screen so nobody scrolls inside a section: no `<section>` may be taller than the viewport at 1440×780 (Daniel's MacBook), and it should also hold at 1280×720, 1512×860 and 1920×1000, in all four languages (German and French run longer). When content does not fit, split it into two sections or lay it out in columns; do not let it grow. The interactive tour on `/restaurants` scales its stage to fit (`.tour-stage` in `globals.css` plus `fitStage` in `interactive-tour.tsx`). Phones are exempt. Measure before shipping any new section.
- `/restaurants` shows the presentation video **on the PT site only** (`public/videos/apresentacao-16x9.mp4` desktop, `-9x16` phones; the narration is Portuguese, so EN/DE/FR skip the section) and the interactive tour (`components/reservations/interactive-tour.tsx`, a client-side replica of the widget + dashboard with fictional guests; copy in `reservations.tour`). The other three business pages share one template (`components/business/business-page.tsx`, copy in `lib/dictionaries/business.<lang>.ts`).
- Hero is the CSS-only "showcase" (`components/home/hero-showcase.tsx`): a phone mock cycling five verticals on keyframes shared with the eyebrow label (4 s per slide). No video. Use CSS-only responsive switching (md:hidden / hidden md:block, container queries) to avoid hydration issues.
- Each business page uses its own wording (tables and guests, stays and guests, appointments and clients, orders and customers); on the homepage and `/pricing` say customers / bookings, orders and sales.
- The booking app (separate repo in `Booking integration/`) is one platform with three products: table reservations (restaurants), stays (hotels, guesthouses, holiday rentals) and appointments (salons, barbers, clinics). The site must only claim what each really does. All three: one-line widget embed, no guest accounts, email confirmation / day-before reminder / cancel link, owner emails, EN/DE/PT/FR, set up by us. Restaurants: day view, floor-plan editor, live floor map. Stays: calendar per unit, seasonal prices and minimum stay, cleaning fee, blocked dates, iCal sync with Airbnb / Booking.com / Vrbo (not instant, dates only). Appointments: service → professional → time, day calendar per professional, services with duration and time after, team hours and time off. **No SMS, no online payment or deposits (Stripe is test-mode only in the app), no Google sync, no waitlist, no self-serve sign-up.** Included in Monthly Professional or sold on its own (Reservations only plan). Login button in the nav/footer points to the dashboard login (`APP_LOGIN_URL`). Domain DNS: CNAME to cname.vercel-dns.com.
- Pricing: six fixed packages in `lib/pricing-data.ts` (Essential, Professional, Online Store, Monthly Essential, Monthly Professional — recommended, Reservations only — one booking platform without a website); plan names localised in the dictionaries. The two monthly website plans include the build with nothing upfront and run on a **12-month contract** (stated on the site); 12 × the monthly price must never be lower than the matching one-time build. "Reservations only" has no minimum term. There is **no money-back guarantee** on any plan (removed 2026-11-11) — never promise a refund. Monthly plans cover hosting and maintenance, not content updates (those are quoted per update on every plan).
- Tone: practical, empathetic, benefit-driven. Never salesy or corporate.
- Do not state competitor figures (commissions, prices) without a source.
- Do not merge to main until told.
